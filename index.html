Founder Initiative

LEUSI, safety as social infrastructure.

A foundation initiative connecting European safety standards, Lebanese frontline realities, and long term capacity building for safer work environments.

Lebanese European Safety Initiative

LEUSI is being developed as a social impact platform focused on HSSE awareness, worker certification, leadership development, and institutional safety culture across Lebanon, with future regional potential.

Training frontline workers and emerging HSSE leaders
Building bridges between European standards and local implementation
Supporting safer construction, transport, logistics, and energy environments

Why it matters

Safety is not only a technical checklist. It is a governance system, a dignity issue, and a form of institutional trust. LEUSI reflects a commitment to translating standards into real protection for people on the ground.
