About Mohamad

Energy transition, infrastructure and public value.

Mohamad Kabbara is an energy transition and infrastructure professional based in The Hague, working across EV mobility, LNG, hydrogen ready infrastructure, urban transformation, public policy and social impact.

With a foundation in architecture and urban design, his work bridges technical delivery, stakeholder governance and public value across Europe, MENA, the GCC and Africa. His practice connects infrastructure execution with human meaning, from multi energy programs and mobility hubs to visual storytelling, policy research and LEUSI, his safety focused social impact initiative.
