About Mohamad

The person who makes it happen.

Mohamad Kabbara delivers complex infrastructure. Not on paper, on the ground, across cultures, institutions, and continents.

Over 15 years he has led capital programmes exceeding €80M for BP and Shell across Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, France, Spain, and Romania, coordinating governments, municipalities, executing agencies, EPCs, grid operators, and suppliers simultaneously. When projects stalled, he was the person sent in to recover them.

His background is rare by design. Architecture and urban planning. An MBA. Public policy at the London School of Economics. Leadership at Harvard Kennedy School. Projects in Europe, the Gulf, and West Africa. Four languages, Arabic natively, English fluently, Dutch professionally, French conversationally.

He is now focused on infrastructure and development impact in emerging markets, where the delivery gap is largest and the stakes are highest.

Arabic · English · Dutch · French · The Hague, available internationally.
